## 🎬 Summary

Some records tell the story of where you have been.  
Others decide where you are allowed to go.

This episode explores **credit protection and identity security** — the quiet systems that determine financial trust in modern life.

It explains how **credit reports work, why identity theft can happen silently, and how a credit freeze can protect your name from unauthorized accounts.**

This is not about panic.  
It is about **ownership of the record written under your name.**

---

## ⚔️ Core Lessons

- Identity theft often happens quietly through financial systems, not visible hacks.
- Credit bureaus maintain the records that determine lending decisions.
- The three major bureaus are **Experian, TransUnion, and Equifax**.
- A **credit freeze** prevents new accounts from being opened without your permission.
- Credit freezes are **free and do not affect your credit score**.
- Freezes can be temporarily lifted when you need to apply for loans, housing, or credit.

---

## 🧭 What a Credit Freeze Does

A credit freeze:

- Locks your credit file from new account creation
- Stops lenders from pulling your credit report
- Prevents fraudsters from opening loans or credit cards in your name

Your existing accounts remain unaffected.

You can lift or re-enable the freeze whenever needed.

---

## 🌐 Where to Freeze Your Credit

A credit freeze must be placed with **all three bureaus**.

Experian  
https://www.experian.com/freeze

TransUnion  
https://www.transunion.com/credit-freeze

Equifax  
https://www.equifax.com/personal/credit-report-services/credit-freeze/

Each bureau allows you to:
- Freeze your credit
- Temporarily lift the freeze
- Permanently remove the freeze if needed
